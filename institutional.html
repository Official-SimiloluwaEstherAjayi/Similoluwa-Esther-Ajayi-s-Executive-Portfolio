Institutional

Systems, not posts

Offices held, and the structures attached to them that were meant to outlast the tenure.

May 2026 to present Afe Babalola University

Director, Opportunity Office

ABUAD Law Students' Society

An office built to make access institutional rather than person‑dependent, so that a student's chance of hearing about a scholarship, an internship or a fellowship does not depend on who they happen to know.

  • Established the Undergraduate and Graduate Opportunity Desks, covering different stages of the student to professional pipeline, together with the tracking, dissemination and handover procedures the office runs on.
  • Designing the ABUAD LSS Opportunity and Professional Development Platform in four layers. Discover, an opportunity database with structured metadata so a student can filter rather than scroll. Prepare, an application readiness lab with review before submission. Develop, a skills programme. Track, an application tracker that follows each submission to its outcome.
  • Two governing decisions precede the build. Access is permanently free, because a student should never pay to discover a fellowship disseminated by their own institution; cost attaches only to advanced training. And no officer owns the platform: the institution owns the infrastructure, the office holds the function, and whoever occupies it operates it.
  • Working toward adoption through the Students' Representative Council across the other ABUAD colleges.

300+ opportunities disseminated. 400+ students reached. 100+ applications supported.
