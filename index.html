Nigeria · Four campuses

Co-founder & Chief Legal and Compliance Officer

MURA — Student Opportunity Discovery Platform

Nigerian students find internships through open tabs and unvetted broadcasts, and those without an existing network never see the roles that are never advertised. MURA aggregates those fragmented channels into a single verified feed and matches openings against a student’s profile.

  • One of four founders drawn from four Nigerian universities. I hold the legal and compliance function: entity structure, data protection, terms of use, listing verification standards, and the framework governing student data as the platform scales.
  • Built from discovery research rather than assumption — a fourteen-student survey across four states, in which nine identified information, access or network barriers as their principal obstacle. Validation across ten campuses is underway.
  • Freemium by design: discovery stays free, with advanced matching, priority alerts and deadline tracking in the paid tier. Working prototype; raising to reach product-market validation.

03 · Institutional Work

Systems, not posts

Offices held, and the structures attached to them that were meant to outlast the tenure.

May 2026 – Present

Afe Babalola University

Director, Opportunity Office

ABUAD Law Students’ Society

An office that makes access to opportunities institutional rather than person-dependent — so that a student’s chance of hearing about a scholarship, internship or fellowship does not depend on who they happen to know.

  • Established the Undergraduate and Graduate Opportunity Desks, covering different stages of the student-to-professional pipeline, and the tracking, dissemination and handover procedures the office runs on.
  • Designing the ABUAD LSS Opportunity & Professional Development Platform: four layers — Discover, an opportunity database with structured metadata so a student can filter rather than scroll; Prepare, an application readiness lab with review before submission; Develop, a skills programme; and Track, an application tracker that follows each submission to outcome.
  • Two governing decisions precede the build. Access is permanently free, because a student should never pay to discover a fellowship disseminated by their own institution; cost attaches only to advanced training. And no officer owns the platform — the institution owns the infrastructure, the office holds the function, and whoever occupies it operates it.
  • Working toward adoption through the Students’ Representative Council across other ABUAD colleges.
  • To date: 300+ opportunities disseminated, 400+ students reached, 100+ applications supported.
