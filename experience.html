August 2026 to present Nigeria, four campuses

Co‑founder, Chief Legal and Compliance Officer

MURA, student opportunity discovery platform

Nigerian students find internships through open tabs and unvetted broadcasts, and those without an existing network never see the roles that are never advertised. MURA gathers those fragmented channels into a single verified feed and matches openings against a student's profile.

  • One of four founders drawn from four Nigerian universities. I hold the legal and compliance function: entity structure, terms of use, listing verification standards, and the framework governing student data as the platform scales.
  • Built from discovery research rather than assumption. A fourteen‑student survey across four states, in which nine identified information, access or network barriers as their principal obstacle. Validation across ten campuses is underway.
  • Freemium by design: discovery stays free, with advanced matching, priority alerts and deadline tracking in the paid tier. Working prototype; raising to reach product‑market validation.
